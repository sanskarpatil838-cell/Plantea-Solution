Garlic Powder Manufacturer & Supplier in India

High-quality dehydrated garlic powder. Made from fresh, selected cloves of garlic. Processed and pulverized in hygienic chambers to safeguard the active essential oils (allicin) that give garlic its characteristic pungent taste and health values.

Technical Specifications

Applications & Uses

  • Commercial Seasonings: Prime flavor component for garlic salt, barbecue rubs, and dry seasoning blends.
  • Snack Seasonings: Widely applied to potato chips, cracker biscuits, namkeens, and cheese puffs.
  • Prepared Foods & Gravies: Adds instant garlic flavor to ready meals, instant noodles, instant soups, and dry dips.
  • Sauce & Dressing Manufacturers: Infuses flavor into pizza sauces, marinades, salad dressings, and mayonnaise blends.

Key Quality Attributes

  • Uniform flavor dispersion in dry mixtures without creating hydration lumps.
  • Eliminates sorting, peeling, and grinding labor in industrial food prep.
  • Concentrated flavor profile allowing economical dosage usage.
  • Long-term ambient stability when sealed against humidity.
