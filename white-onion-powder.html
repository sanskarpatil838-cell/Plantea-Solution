Technical Specifications

Ingredient 100% Dehydrated White Onion (Allium cepa)
Form Fine Powder (no lumps)
Moisture Content < 5.0% (Max)
Mesh Size 80 - 100 Mesh
Color / Appearance Creamish White to pale yellow
Preservatives Zero anti-caking agents, synthetic flow agents, or fillers
Packaging Options 20 Kg / 25 Kg corrugated carton boxes with internal moisture-proof liners

Applications & Uses

  • Ready-to-eat Foods: Convenient flavor enhancer for dry noodles, instant soups, and pasta premixes.
  • Snack Coatings: Standard flavoring ingredient dusted over chips, crackers, popcorn, and namkeens.
  • Sauces & Gravies: Adds body, consistency, and onion flavor profile to dry gravy powders, sauces, and dips.
  • Seasoning Blends: Key base component in compound spices like onion-garlic powder, curry powder, and taco mixes.

Key Health Benefits & Qualities

  • Retains natural onion bio-nutrients, minerals, and flavor compounds.
  • Highly hygroscopic properties, allowing easy blending in dry formulations.
  • No peeling or cutting required, reducing labor costs in commercial kitchens.
  • Extended shelf-life of 12+ months when stored in moisture-sealed conditions.
