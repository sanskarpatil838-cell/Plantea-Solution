Technical Specifications

Ingredient 100% Dehydrated Red Onion (Allium cepa L.)
Form Free-flowing powder
Moisture Content < 5.0% (Max)

Packaging Options 20 Kg / 25 Kg moisture barrier HDPE bags with food-grade inner PE liners

Applications & Uses

  • Spice and Masala Blends: Commonly added to dry masala premixes, seasoning seasonings, and barbecue flavor bases.
  • Savory Snacks: Dusted on potato chips, corn crisps, puff loops, and fried nuts.
  • Ready-to-Cook Products: Key flavoring ingredient in instant soups, biryani mixes, and ready meal mixes.
  • Processed Food Industry: Serves as an essential flavoring base in sauces, meat rubs, and canned foods.

Key Health Benefits & Qualities

  • Loaded with active antioxidants, including quercetin and anthocyanin color compounds.
  • Concentrated sweet-savory flavor profile requiring less dosage in bulk mixes.
  • Reduces kitchen preparation labor (no peeling, tearing, or chopping).
  • High stability under proper B2B packaging and dry conditions.
