Pure Purifier • Herbal Grade • Bioactive Rich

Dehydrated Herbal Powder

Neem Leaf Powder Manufacturer & Supplier in India

High-purity dehydrated Neem leaf powder. Processed from freshly collected Azadirachta indica leaves. Triple washed, dried in shadow-dehydration docks to maintain active organic compounds (such as azadirachtin), and milled to fine mesh sizes. Ideal for cosmetic, veterinary, and pharmaceutical compounding.

Applications & Uses

  • Cosmetic Formulations: Key botanical ingredient in purifying face washes, herbal masks, anti-acne formulations, and soaps.
  • Herbal Formulations: Used in traditional wellness supplements, blood purifiers, and organic capsules.
  • Natural Care & Skincare: Infused into dandruff control shampoos, scalp ointments, and body washes.
  • Organic Agriculture: Used as biological inputs, plant micro-sprays, and soil pest deterrents.

Key Quality Attributes

  • Strong bitter aroma characteristic of natural fresh neem.
  • Processed under strict temperatures to safeguard active azadirachtin contents.
  • Triple-sieved to ensure fine, clump-free mixing in liquid or clay bases.
  • Free from heavy metal contaminants and pesticide residues.
