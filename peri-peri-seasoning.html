Applications & Uses

  • Fried Snacks & Chips: Direct dusting on potato chips, banana chips, and extruded puffs.
  • QSR & Restaurant Chains: Perfect seasoning shaker for French fries, onion rings, and fried chicken.

  • Dips & Marinades: Easily disperses in mayonnaise, yogurt, or oil bases for spicy specialty sauces.
