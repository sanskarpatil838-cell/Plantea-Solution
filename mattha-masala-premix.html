Mattha Masala Premix / Buttermilk Masala Supplier

Traditional digestive spice premix optimized for Buttermilk and Chaas products. Crafted from high-purity roasted cumin, black salt, dehydrated mint, ginger, and green chili powder. Extensively used in dairy plants, bottling units, and commercial kitchens.

Technical Specifications

Ingredient Base Cumin, Black Salt, Mint, Ginger, Coriander, Salt, spices
Form Blended powdered seasoning
Moisture Content < 5.0% (Max)
Solubility / Dispersion Excellent dispersion in yogurt and buttermilk liquids
Color / Appearance Pale greenish brown powder
Adhesion & Flow Free-flowing powder compound
Packaging Options 20 Kg / 25 Kg moisture-proof cartons with food-grade inner PE liners

Applications & Uses

  • Dairy Industries: Added during buttermilk batching to make packaged commercial "Spiced Chaas" or "Mattha".
  • Instant Drink Premixes: Dry mix base for single-serve sachet manufacturers.
  • HORECA Procurements: Instant flavoring powder for hotels, wedding catering, and institutional kitchens.
  • Spice Brands: Repacked directly into small shaker jars or pouches for retail distribution.

Key Quality Attributes

  • Uniform flavor dispersion preventing dry spice sedimentation in beverages.
  • Balanced sodium-potassium and digestive profile.
  • Saves immense compounding labor and maintains consistency across factory batches.
  • Contains 100% natural dehydrated botanicals; no synthetic coloring or thickening agents.
