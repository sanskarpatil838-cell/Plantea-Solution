Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common business, logistical, and technical enquiries regarding our supply capacities.

Do you provide bulk supply?
Yes, Plantea Solution is a dedicated commercial B2B manufacturer and bulk supplier. We cater to domestic and international clients with batch consistency and scalability, managing order sizes ranging from small test pallets to multiple metric tons.
Can you create customized seasoning blends?
Absolutely. We provide customized formulation services. You can specify flavor notes (spiciness, sweetness, umami levels), application substrate (chips, pellets, nuts, instant soups), target price-points, and technical constraints (gluten-free, allergen-free). Our lab will create a pilot match for your review.
Do you supply to food manufacturing companies?
Yes, food manufacturing companies are our core clientele. We serve snack manufacturers, dry spice blenders, tea packers, health-supplement makers, instant noodle manufacturers, bakery processors, and large HORECA procurement agencies.
What packaging options are available?
Our standard packaging includes heavy-duty multi-layer paper bags with inner food-grade PE liner bags (typically 20kg to 25kg bags) or corrugated boxes for seasonings. Customized packaging configurations, drum packaging, or custom weight packaging can be arranged on contract terms.
How can I send a product enquiry?
You can navigate to our Contact Page to submit an online enquiry form specifying the quantity and specific powder you require. Alternatively, you can click on the WhatsApp button on the page to chat instantly or email us directly at planteasolution.2505@gmail.com.
Can I request product samples?
Yes, we encourage sample evaluation. We supply free product samples (within nominal weight limits) to registered commercial food businesses, processors, and R&D labs. Courier delivery charges are generally managed by the buyer or adjusted upon placing a bulk order.
