About Plantea Solution

Pioneering the manufacture of high-purity dehydrated powders, flavor seasonings, and custom ingredients for commercial food processing and global distribution.

B2B Bulk Capacity

Operating from Jalgaon, Maharashtra, India. Engineered for raw material consistency and international logistics standards.

World-Class Dehydration Standards

Plantea Solution is a recognized manufacturer, wholesaler, and exporter specializing in dehydrated fruit powders, vegetable powders, herbal powders, food ingredients, seasonings, masala premixes, and customized blends.

We source our raw inputs directly from fertile local farms in Maharashtra, validating pesticide-free growth patterns. Utilizing progressive convective hot-air dehydration chambers, we manage thermal exposures to prevent nutrient degradation and oxidation, keeping active colorant levels, natural aromas, and chemical attributes fully intact.

Our commitment remains fixed on catering to bulk purchasers, food scientists, and packagers with standard batch-to-batch traceability, customized formulation, and stable pricing models.

Our Mission

To supply global food manufacturers, snack blenders, and pharmaceutical companies with natural, premium-grade dehydrated ingredients that optimize production efficiency, preserve shelf life, and deliver exceptional organoleptic profiles without relying on chemical stabilizers.

Our Vision

To be established as the leading, export-ready manufacturer of choice for custom dehydrated raw ingredients, recognized for maintaining strict clean-label standards, cutting-edge R&D formulations, and sustainable agricultural partnerships across India and global B2B networks.

Our Core Pillars of Excellence

Quality is not a final checkpoint; it is embedded throughout our manufacturing process.

Rigorous R&D Testing

We analyze physical moisture content, particle mesh distribution, and volatile oil retention before any batch is packed for delivery.

Hygienic Clean Rooms

Sorting, dehydrating, and milling take place inside temperature-controlled processing zones to prevent microbial growth.

Secure Global Logistics

Standardized moisture barrier packing options ensure our bulk products survive shipping transit and ambient storage safely.

Clean Labels

No synthetic color enhancers, flavor powders, anti-caking compounds, or chemicals are added to our single-ingredient powders.

Export-Ready Supply Chain

Whether you are a food processor in Europe, a snack manufacturer in India, or a distributor in the Middle East, our team manages customs logistics, shipping document templates, and bulk packaging specifications seamlessly.
