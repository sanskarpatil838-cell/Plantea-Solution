About Plantea Solution

A professional B2B manufacturer, exporter, supplier and trader focused on dehydrated powders, herbal powders, food ingredients, seasonings, masala premixes and customized blends.

Company introduction

Quality-focused food ingredient solutions from Jalgaon, Maharashtra.

Plantea Solution supplies a wide range of dehydrated green banana powder, beetroot powder, garlic powder, white onion powder, tomato powder, mint powder, neem powder, moringa powder, seasoning products and mattha masala powder.

Under the leadership of Mr. Ganesh Sanjay Jaware and Mrs. Sarika Borase, the company works with food businesses that need practical ingredient support, clear product communication and bulk order discussions within promised time frames.
