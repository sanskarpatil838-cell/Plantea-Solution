Mint Powder

Pure Pudina • Refreshing Menthol • High Aroma

Dehydrated Herbal Powder

Mint Powder Manufacturer & Supplier

High-quality dehydrated Mint (Pudina) powder. Harvested from high-aroma field crops, washed thoroughly, hot-air dried inside temperature-monitored tunnels, and ground into fine aromatic particles. Retains the essential cooling menthol component and deep green color profile. Great for beverages and snack flavorings.

Applications & Uses

  • Beverage Premixes: Ideal cooling additive for mint lemonade, instant jaljeera, mocktails, and green teas.
  • Spice Masala Blends: Added to chat masala, panipuri masala, buttermilk seasoning, and salad dressings.
  • Chutneys & Instant Foods: Extensively used by instant food brands to create fast chutney mixes, dips, and snack seasoning.
  • Confectionery: Natural flavoring agent in mint candies, digestive tablets, and mouth fresheners.

Key Quality Attributes

  • Retains distinct fresh minty aroma and refreshing tongue-cooling properties.
  • Extended shelf-life of 12+ months when kept away from moisture and direct light.
  • Purity guaranteed; absolutely free from coloring powders or artificial starches.
  • Finely milled to dissolve smoothly in dry or wet formulas.
